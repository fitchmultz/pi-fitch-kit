Before considering this task complete, reflect on everything that occurred in this conversation and determine whether there are any durable improvements that should be incorporated into this project's `AGENTS.md`.

Your objective is to continuously improve future agent performance, not to document this conversation.

Guidelines:

* Extract general principles, workflows, heuristics, and verification practices rather than incident-specific instructions.
* Prefer strengthening existing guidance over creating new rules whenever possible.
* Do not add project-specific trivia, one-off exceptions, or reminders that are unlikely to matter again.
* If the conversation exposed a missing workflow, weak verification step, poor decision process, or useful engineering practice, codify the underlying principle instead of the specific example.
* If no durable improvement is warranted, make no changes.
* Keep the guidance concise, high signal, and broadly applicable.

Before modifying `AGENTS.md`, ask yourself:

* Will this improve future work beyond this specific task?
* Does this describe a repeatable behavior rather than a single incident?
* Is this guidance something a highly competent engineer would naturally want available on future tasks?
* Can this be expressed as a general rule instead of an example?

If changes are warranted:

1. Read the existing `AGENTS.md` completely.
2. Integrate the new guidance into the most appropriate section, improving or consolidating existing rules where possible instead of creating redundant entries.
3. Keep the document cohesive, concise, and free of duplication.
4. Explain exactly what changed and why.

Treat `AGENTS.md` as a living engineering playbook whose quality is more important than its size.
